Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. We are committed to protecting your data with transparent practices.

Last Updated: January 1, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Free Tools Hub ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we handle information when you use our website and tools.

🔒 Key Principle: All our tools process data directly in your browser. Images, text, and files you upload NEVER leave your device. We do not have servers that store your content.

2. Information We Collect

Since our tools process everything locally, we collect minimal information:

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the limited anonymous data we collect for:

  • Service Improvement: Understanding which tools are most popular to enhance functionality.
  • Performance Monitoring: Ensuring our website loads quickly and works correctly across devices.
  • Security: Protecting against abuse and maintaining website integrity.
  • Analytics: Aggregate, anonymous statistics about visitor behavior (via privacy-respecting tools).

4. Tool-Specific Privacy Notes

Each tool is designed with privacy in mind. Here's how specific tools handle your data:

🔐 Password Generator

All passwords are generated locally in your browser. No passwords are ever transmitted or stored.

🗜️ Image Compressor

Images are processed entirely on your device using HTML5 Canvas. Files never leave your computer.

📝 Word Counter

Text you type stays in your browser. No data is sent to any server.

📄 PDF to Word & QR Generator

File processing occurs locally. For PDF conversion, your document never uploads to any external service.

🔍 Plagiarism Checker

Text comparison uses built-in reference data. Your input text is not stored or transmitted.

🎵 YouTube Tag Generator

Keywords are used only to generate suggestions locally. No search history is saved.

5. Cookies & Tracking

We use minimal, essential cookies only:

  • Session Cookies: Temporary cookies that help the website function properly.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your preferences (like theme selection).

We do NOT use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party tracking scripts. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work optimally.

6. Third-Party Services

We use the following privacy-respecting third-party services:

  • Google Fonts: Used for typography (no tracking).
  • QR Code Library: Client-side QR generation (qrcodejs) - no external calls.

We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook pixels, or any tracking scripts that collect personal data.

7. Data Security

Since we don't store your data, security risks are minimal. However, we implement:

  • HTTPS Encryption: All connections are encrypted.
  • No Server Storage: Your content never reaches our servers.
  • Regular Updates: We keep our codebase updated for security.

8. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliant)

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Right to Access: Request information about data we hold (we hold minimal to none).
  • Right to Deletion: Since we don't store data, nothing needs deletion.
  • Right to Opt-out: You can disable cookies in browser settings.
  • Right to Information: Understand how your data is processed.

To exercise any rights, contact us at the email below.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. All tools are safe for educational use.
